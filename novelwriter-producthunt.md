## First Comment / Maker Comment

Hi Product Hunt,

I'm building NovelWriter for writers who have a scene, a character, or a messy idea in their head, but need help turning it into a full story.

NovelWriter is designed for fiction-first creation: rich character setup, plot templates, genre-aware story planning, and chapter drafting that keeps the world and relationships consistent as the story grows.

It's especially useful for romance, fantasy, paranormal, drama, and character-driven stories where emotional tension matters as much as plot structure.

What you can do with NovelWriter:

- Start from a premise, trope, or template
- Build characters with motivations, secrets, and relationships
- Generate structured plot foundations
- Draft chapters with continuity in mind
- Explore different story directions without losing the core idea

The goal is not to make writing feel automatic. It's to make the blank page feel alive faster.

I'd love feedback from writers, readers, and anyone building creative tools.
